Last updated 25 November 2026

MAE 494/564 Advanced Aerodynamics, Fall 2026. On October 7 the class replaced Homeworks 3 to 5 with one shared research project: six teams benchmark low-cost aerodynamic tools on the same airfoil against the same wind-tunnel data, and a seventh team validates full-car CFD for Sun Devil Motorsports. This page traces each team from its first proposal to where it stands today.

What we have learned so far

  • Viscosity fixes most of the lift errorThe inviscid panel code is 12.5% high in lift; XFOIL and NeuralFoil cut that to about 7%.
  • A laminar region fixes the dragFully turbulent CFD is 20–25% high in drag; the transition model brings Fluent within about 1%.
  • Two codes agree, so the model is the limitFluent and STAR-CCM+ with matched settings agree within 3–5% in drag, so the remaining error comes from the turbulence model, not the code.
  • A surrogate inherits its teacher's errorsNeuralFoil matches XFOIL to within 0.5%, including where XFOIL is wrong.
