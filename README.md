# Research Studio Journey

Educational page for MAE 494/564 Advanced Aerodynamics (Fall 2026), School for Engineering of Matter, Transport and Energy, Arizona State University, prepared by the course instructor.

It summarizes student research in progress; results are preliminary and not peer reviewed. Wind-tunnel data are cited from NASA Langley (TM-4062) and the University of Illinois (UIUC) and remain with their sources. Formula SAE results belong to Sun Devil Motorsports.
